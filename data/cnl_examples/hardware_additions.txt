Tactic: TA0001 "Initial Access"
Technique: T1200 "Hardware Additions"

Background
Given "operating system" as "Windows" 
And "device" as "D"
And "user session" as "S"
And "process" as "P"
And "server" as "DHCP"

Event 1
Given "Windows is running"
When "Windows recognizes external D of type USB or Thunderbolt or PCIe under S"
Then "external D is connected to Windows"

Event 2
Given "Windows is running"
When "Windows recognizes a new block device BD under S"
Then "BD is connected to Windows in S"

Event 3
Given "Event 1" Or "Event 2"
When "parent P spawns new process P1 under S"
Then "P1 is running in S"

Event 4
Given "Event 1" Or "Event 2"
When "Windows mounts a new volume V under S"
Then "V is mounted on Windows in S"

Event 5
Given "Event 3" Or "Event 4"
When "Windows records a HID keystroke under S"
Then "HID keystroke injected in S"

Event 6
Given "Event 3" Or "Event 4"
When "Windows loads DMA driver under S"
Then "DMA driver loaded in S"

Event 7
Given "Event 3" Or "Event 4"
When "DHCP receives request to create new network interface MAC"
Then "MAC is added on DHCP"

Event 8
Given "Event 3" Or "Event 4" Or "Event 5" Or "Event 6" Or "Event 7"
Then "possible attack"
