Tactic: TA0006 Credential_Access
Technique: T1110.001 Brute_Force_Password_Guessing

Background
Given service as AuthService
And account as TargetAccount
And credentials as Credentials

Event 1
Given AuthService is active
When AuthService rejects Credentials by TargetAccount
Then Credentials is rejected by AuthService

Event 2
Given Event 1
When AuthService accepts Credentials by TargetAccount
Then Credentials is accepted by AuthService

Detection
Event 1 And Event 2
