Tactic: TA0001 Initial_Access
Technique: T1659 Content_Injection

Background
Given browser as B
And folder as AppData
And folder as Temp
And process as P
And file as F

Event 1
Given B is active
When B spawns P
Then P is active

Event 2
Given P is active
When P creates F in AppData
Then F exists in AppData And P is active

Event 3
Given P is active
When P creates F in Temp
Then F exists in Temp And P is active

Event 4
Given F exists in AppData
And P is active
When P executes F in AppData
Then F is active
 
Event 5
Given F exists in Temp
And P is active
When P executes F in Temp
Then F is active

Detection
Event 4 Or Event 5
