Tactic: TA0001 "Initial Access"
Technique: T1190 "Exploit Public-Facing Application"

Background
Given "application" as "App" 
And "public endpoint" as "E"
And "server" as "S"

Event 1
Given "App is running" And "App contains E" And "E is connected to S"
When "E sends abnormal request to S"
Then "abnormal requests received by S"

Event 2
Given "Event 1"
When "S raises multiple 4xx/5xx errors"
Then "4xx/5xx errors are elevated"

Event 3
Given "Event 1"
When "S executes unusual methods/paths"
Then "unusual methods/paths are executed"

Event 4
Given "Event 2" Or "Event 3"
When "S spawns shell/LOLbins"
Then "shell/LOLbins spawned by S"
And "shell/LOLbins are running"

Event 5
Given "Event 2" Or "Event 3"
When "S loads non-standard modules"
Then "non-standard modules loaded spawned by S"

Event 6
Given "Event 4" Or "Event 5"
When "S sends outbound callback"
Then "outbound callback sent by S"

Event 7
Given "Event 4" Or "Event 5" Or "Event 6"
Then "possible attack"
